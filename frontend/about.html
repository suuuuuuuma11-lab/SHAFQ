About SHAFQ

Before the lights fade.

More than what you wear — garments shaped for the quiet hour between dusk and dark.

The Meaning of Shafaq

Between daylight and nightfall.

Named after the Arabic word for twilight, SHAFQ lives in the brief stillness when warm light lingers on stone and shadow stretches across the room.

Every silhouette balances structured tailoring with effortless ease—built in considered runs with tactile fabrics, understated embroidery, and a palette anchored in ink and warm cream.

“Designed to carry presence quietly, long after the lights soften.”
